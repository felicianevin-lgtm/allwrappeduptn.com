Why hire a professional

Presentation is part of the gift

The average household spends six or more hours wrapping each December. For a business, the job is measured in days and usually lands on the person who can least spare them.

Reclaim your time

Hand over the entire order and spend the season with the people the gifts are for.

A finished look

Crisp corners, concealed seams, real ribbon and hand-tied bows. Every gift is tagged and ready to present.

On brand, every time

Consistent wrapping across every gift in the order, in your colors, with your logo on the tag.

Pickup and delivery
