Occasions

Gift wrapping for weddings, showers, birthdays, anniversaries & holidays

One gift or one hundred. Every occasion receives coordinated paper, real ribbon, a hand-tied bow and a tag, wrapped by hand in Sweetwater, Tennessee.

Weddings

Bridesmaid and groomsmen gifts, parent gifts, welcome bags for out-of-town guests and the gift you're bringing to someone else's big day.

  • Ribbon matched to your wedding palette
  • Welcome-bag assembly and tagging
  • Delivery to the venue or hotel block

Bridal showers

Host gifts, favors and shower gifts wrapped to match the theme, so the gift table looks styled and photographs beautifully.

  • Favor wrapping in bulk
  • Coordinated gift-table display
  • Registry gifts received and wrapped for you

Baby showers

Large boxes, unusual shapes and the smallest keepsakes, wrapped in soft palettes or the nursery colors the parents have chosen.

  • Oversized item specialists
  • Gender-reveal wrapping handled discreetly
  • Diaper cakes and gift baskets finished

Birthdays

Milestone birthdays, children's parties and surprise gifts. Bright and playful or quietly elegant, wrapped to suit the recipient.

  • Durable wrapping that travels well
  • Themed paper and ribbon
  • Same-week turnaround when available

Anniversaries

Luxury paper, double-satin ribbon and a handwritten card, finished well ahead of the date.

  • Handwritten note cards
  • Discreet pickup and delivery

What's included

Every gift, every time

  • Premium paper — heavyweight, opaque, coordinated to the occasion or your colors
  • Real ribbon — grosgrain, satin, velvet or our signature polka dot, never curling plastic
  • Hand-tied bow — full, even and secured so it survives the trip
  • Gift tag — printed or handwritten with the recipient's name
  • Crisp corners & hidden tape — the details people notice
Unusual shapes welcome

Baskets, bicycles, instruments, plush toys and bottles are all wrapped regularly. Send a photograph for an exact quote.

Your own materials
