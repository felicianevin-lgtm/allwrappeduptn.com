Pricing

Gift wrapping prices & packages

Two levels of wrapping, priced clearly. Classic wrapping is priced by the group with all materials included. Signature custom wraps are quoted by the gift, because the materials and the time are more.

Classic wrap

Priced by the group, all materials included

Paper, coordinating ribbon, a bow and a gift tag on every gift, in any color scheme or theme you like. Standard-size gifts; oversized items are quoted.

GroupGood forPrice
Small group1 – 10 giftsOne person's list, a shower gift or two, the special few$60
Medium group11 – 25 giftsThe whole family, grandparents and teachers included$80
Large group26 – 50 giftsLarge households and the home everyone gathers in$100
Extra large group51+ giftsBig families, class parties, office exchangesfrom $120

Prices include all wrapping materials. Oversized and odd-shaped items add from $15 each. Gift cards can be dressed up in a small box with ribbon and a tag for $5 each.

Signature custom wrap

Themed and custom builds, quoted by the gift

The shirt-and-tie for Dad, the tulle-topped baby shower set, the box that matches the party invitation. Custom wraps use specialty ribbon, tulle, mesh, toppers and hand-built details, and take real time, so they cost more than a Classic wrap.
