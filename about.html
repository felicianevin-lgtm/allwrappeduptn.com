About us

Meet Amiebeth, the hands behind the bows

All Wrapped Up is a home-based gift wrapping studio in Sweetwater, Tennessee, run by one person who genuinely loves crisp corners and a full bow.

A little about the studio

Hi, I'm Amiebeth Thearp. I started All Wrapped Up because every December I was the person friends and family handed their gifts to. Somewhere between the third Christmas of wrapping for the whole street and the first office that asked me to do their client gifts, it turned into a business.

I work from a dedicated studio at home in Sweetwater, right on I-75 between Knoxville and Chattanooga. That means I can pick up from a downtown Knoxville office in the morning and deliver to a Chattanooga party venue the same week. Every gift is wrapped by hand, by me, with paper and ribbon I've chosen because they hold a crease and tie a proper bow.

What I care about

  • The details. Hidden tape, aligned patterns, bows that stay full through the car ride.
  • Your colors, not mine. Corporate orders match your brand. Family orders match your tree.
  • Being easy to work with. Quick replies, clear pricing, and I show up when I say I will.
Fun fact.

The signature pink polka-dot ribbon on my business card is the same one I tie on the very first gift of every order. It's my way of signing the work.
